# Math Rush — how a game runs

A fast, whole-class race. Four number-sentence cards are on screen at once;
every team works on all of them at the same time. Available on maths topics
with equation practice. 2–6 teams, about 10 minutes.

## Starting the game

1. From the **Library**, pick a maths topic and choose **Math Rush**.
2. On the **Game setup** screen, read the *How to play* rules, set the team
   count, names and colours.
3. Press **Start Game** — after the countdown, the first four cards are dealt.

## Each round

1. Four cards appear, each showing a number sentence with **one missing
   number** (for example `325 + ? = 525`) and a points value — harder cards
   are worth more.
2. All teams race to solve any card. When a team calls out an answer, tap
   that card, then tap the team in the sidebar — the card locks in their
   colour and they bank the points.
   - Use **Show answer** on a card (or **Reveal all answers** on the action
     bar) whenever you want to check or discuss.
3. When every card is claimed, press **Next round** for a fresh set of
   cards. The header shows **Round X of Y** so everyone can see how far
   along the game is.

## Ending

On the final round the button becomes **Finish** — pressing it shows the
podium with final scores. **End game** finishes early at any point.

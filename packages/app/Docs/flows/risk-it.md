# Risk It — how a game runs

Confidence wagering: every team wagers on how sure they are before each
question is revealed. Best for 2–6 teams, about 10–15 minutes.

## Starting the game

1. From the **Library**, pick any topic and choose **Risk It**.
2. On the **Game setup** screen, read the *How to play* rules, set the team
   count (2–6), names and colours.
3. Press **Start Game** — after the countdown, every team starts with a
   bank of **10** points and the first wager stage appears.

## Each question

1. The screen shows only the subtopic (for example "Fossils"). Each team
   taps its row in the sidebar to cycle its wager: **1 → 3 → 5**.
2. Once every team has wagered, press **Show question** to reveal the full
   question.
3. Read it aloud, then press **Reveal answer**.
4. Tap **every team that got it right** in the sidebar — each tap toggles
   that team — then press **Next**. Right answers add the wager to that
   team's bank; wrong answers lose it (a bank never drops below 0).
5. The final question always forces every team to wager 5 — a banner makes
   this clear on screen.

## Ending

After the tenth question, the podium ranks teams by their final bank.
**Back to Library** returns to the topic list; **End game** finishes early
at any time.

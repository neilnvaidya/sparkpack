# Strategy Board Quiz — how a game runs

A Jeopardy-style board for reviewing a whole topic. Teams pick questions,
earn points, and can steal from each other. Best for 3–5 teams, about 10–15
minutes.

## Starting the game

1. From the **Library**, pick your subject, year and topic.
2. On the topic page, choose **Strategy Board Quiz**.
3. On the **Game setup** screen:
   - Read the *How to play* rules with the class.
   - Set the number of teams (3–5), and optionally type team names and tap
     the swatches to change team colours.
4. Press **Start Game** — a 3-2-1 countdown plays and the board appears.

## Each turn

1. The header shows whose turn it is. The active team picks any open cell —
   columns are subtopics, and higher points mean harder questions.
2. The question opens with a **20-second discussion timer**. The team talks
   quietly, then gives one answer out loud.
3. You judge the answer:
   - **Correct** — the team wins the points and the cell locks in their
     colour. Play passes to the next team.
   - **Incorrect** — the other teams each get a quick **5-second steal**
     chance, in turn order. A successful steal wins the points.
4. Use **Reveal answers** on the teacher bar if you want to show the
   accepted answers to the class.

## Ending

When every cell has been played, the podium appears with the winning team
and final scores. Use **Back to Library** to pick another game, or **End
game** at any time to finish early.
